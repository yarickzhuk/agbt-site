The story

At first, there was nothing.

Then the White Universal Crystal formed, made of eight aspects: Speed, Creativity, Spirit, Life, Control, Resistance, Matter and Power. A thousand years later, a character called Delta got bored of living in monochrome. He struck the crystal and broke it apart.

You are what came out of it: a piece of the Crystal of Spirit that absorbed a few healing orbs and woke up as a little yellow ship. Now it's your job to take the crystals back.

Colour is the rulebook

The eight aspects

Every colour in the game means something. The four warm aspects are your weapons and your health. The four cool ones Delta took, and they tell you how to fight anything you meet.

Warm colours: yours

Speed
The melee weapon, LINE SLASHER.
Creativity
Bounces off things: BOUNCY BALL.
Spirit
You, your ship and your shots.
Life
Heals you. Every healing orb.

Cool colours: Delta's

Control
Breaks to any shot.
Resistance
Only a charged shot breaks it.
Matter
Never breaks. Don't touch it.
Power
Hits you twice as hard.

Tap to shoot, hold to charge

Four weapons

Charge Cannon

Tap for shots, hold for torpedoes, hold longer for a supercharge.

Cyber Shotgun

A close-range blast that charges into an orbiting Bomb Shield you can throw.

Bouncy Ball

Shots that ricochet off everything.

Line Slasher

A melee slash, and a dash that cuts through anything in its way.

No checkpoints, real lives

After the credits

Alternative game modes

Endless: random attacks and a boss every round, until you fall. Speedrun: the whole game in 35 minutes, one life, against your own record.

5 ship costumes

Each with its own passive ability, and a downside to match.

35 achievements

From your first clear to the full tryhard ones.

Toxic Mode (12+)

Turn it on and the game roasts you out loud, with a different voice line for every way you can die.

Hitbox on death

The game freezes and shows exactly what hit you.
