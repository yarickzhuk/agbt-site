Particles

Everything in the game is forged from particles, from the quecton up to the subons below zero.

The Quantum Codex: primordial

  • Quectons (q0): the smallest particles there are. Nothing smaller exists, so they can never decay. Everything in light and matter is forged from them.
  • Subons (λ): particles that exist below zero. Drain all light from empty space and what remains is less than nothing: the negative void behind Delta and every hazard in your way.
  • Photinos (γ̃) and positrons (e+): the dark opposites of your photons (γ) and electrons (e−). They form Delta's body, which is why he mirrors your every weapon.

The Quantum Codex: the player and time

  • Tachyons (τ): faster-than-light particles. They power your movement and let your dash slip through hazards untouched.
  • Sterile neutrinos (νs): ghost particles that ignore all barriers, letting your Bomb Shield and attacks phase straight through solid walls.
  • Chronons (χ): indivisible quanta of time. The story happens before the first chronon was born.

The Quantum Codex: subon numbers

  • Chameleon particles (λ1): shift by environment. They govern the colour rules and Showman's invincible orange state.
  • Magnetic monopoles (λ2): single-pole particles that lock onto you to guide homing attacks.
  • Symmetrons (λ3): shrinking traps and force fields.
  • Graviphotons (λ4): violent ricochets.
  • Dilatons (λ5): warp space into 4D boss shapes. (See the force fields on the cool crystals.)
  • Mirror matter (λ6): flips your screen, sound and colours backwards.
