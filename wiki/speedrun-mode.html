Speedrun Mode

The whole game against the clock: every stage and Outer Space in a row, with one life.

UnlockBeat the game
WhereTitle screen, Alternative Game Modes
DifficultyEasy
LivesOne for the whole run
Start time35:00
WeaponsAll four, switch with V
PauseNone

How to start it

Speedrun Mode and Endless Mode share the Alternative Game Modes row of the title screen, which appears once you beat the game. Pick Speedrun Mode with the up and down arrows and press Z or Space to play (on a phone, tap it). X goes back. The line under the list shows its rules and your record: Easy, 1 life, the time limit, and your record time.

How it works

You play every stage in a row, then Outer Space, against the clock.

  • There are no title cards, and no pause.
  • It plays on Easy, but you have one life for the whole run.
  • You heal to full between phases.
  • You have all four weapons from the start. Switch with V.

The clock

You start with 35:00. If time runs out, you die. Beat the record and the limit becomes your time plus 1 second, so every run is against your own best.
