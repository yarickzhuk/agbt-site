Weapon

Cyber Shotgun

The Cyber Shotgun: A close-range blast that charges into an orbiting Bomb Shield you can throw.

Given byCyber Zoo
Pellets3 per shot, 2 damage each
Reload0.6 seconds
MovementHalf speed

How it works

  • Tap: 3 fast pellets, 2 damage each. Pellets kill dark blue without charging, but break on purple, so shoot around it.
  • Reload: 0.6 seconds after every volley. You move at half speed with it.
  • Hold 1.5 seconds and let go: The Bomb Shield.

Tip: Point-blank, all 3 pellets hit.

Colour rules

Cyan is destroyed by any shot, dark blue only by a charged shot, purple can't be destroyed, and pink can't be destroyed and hits for 2.
