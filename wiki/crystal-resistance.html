Crystal of Resistance

The Crystal of Resistance is one of the eight aspects of the White Universal Crystal. It is one of the four cool crystals, which Delta took.

GroupCool colours (Delta's)
ColourDark blue
ParticlesCharm quarks
WhereThe Finale
In the gameOnly a charged shot breaks it.
Force field5-cell

Story

Delta forged the four cool crystals into one. In the Finale, each of them sits inside a force field shaped like a spinning 4D shape. The force field of Resistance is the 5-cell.

In the game

The colour dark blue means: Only a charged shot breaks it.

Force field: the 5-cell

The Finale's phases 2 to 5 are the four force fields of the cool crystals. Each is a spinning 4D shape, and its lines hurt to touch. The gaps between the lines are safe.

  • 5-cell: 200 HP, and only charged hits hurt it.
  • Behaviour: It fires bouncing rings.

Tip: a shot that slips through a gap and hits the crystal inside still counts.
