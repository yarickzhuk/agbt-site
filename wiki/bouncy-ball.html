Weapon

Bouncy Ball

The Bouncy Ball: Shots that ricochet off everything.

Given byCrazy Carnival
CrystalCreativity: you absorb its quarks
Ball limitYour max HP
Repeat hitOnce per 0.6 seconds

How it works

  • Tap: A ball that bounces off what it hits, killing what it can. It ends at the screen edge.
  • Hold and let go: An orange ball that kills dark blue and bounces off the edges too. Against purple and walls, it just ricochets.
  • Limits: No more balls on screen than your max HP. A ball hits the same target once per 0.6 seconds.

Tip: Fire into a crowd or a corridor and let the bounces do the work.

Colour rules

Cyan is destroyed by any shot, dark blue only by a charged shot, purple can't be destroyed, and pink can't be destroyed and hits for 2.
