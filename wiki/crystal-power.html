Crystal of Power

The Crystal of Power is one of the eight aspects of the White Universal Crystal. It is one of the four cool crystals, which Delta took.

Story

Delta seized the Crystal of Power first, then captured the other cool crystals and forged them into one. In the Finale, the force field of Power is the 120-cell.

Strange matter turns whatever it touches into more of itself. That is why pink hits you twice as hard.

In the game

The colour pink means: Hits you twice as hard.

Force field: the 120-cell

The Finale's phases 2 to 5 are the four force fields of the cool crystals. Each is a spinning 4D shape, and its lines hurt to touch. The gaps between the lines are safe.

  • 120-cell: There is no HP to beat: survive for a minute.
  • Behaviour: It follows you.

Tip: a shot that slips through a gap and hits the crystal inside still counts.
