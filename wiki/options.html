Settings you adjust

Open Options from the title screen. Change these rows in place with the left and right arrows.

  • Volume
  • Resolution: the screen size.
  • Display: windowed, borderless or fullscreen. Borderless is the default.
  • Orientation: horizontal, vertical or auto. This row only appears on phones and tablets.
  • Screen border: draws the game's art (Kangunroo, Plasmadrache and their names) in the empty bars around the game. It's on by default, except on a phone whose side bars are too narrow for the joystick and fire button, where it starts off. Changing it reloads the game.
  • Toxic Mode (12+): on or off. See below.
  • Death hitbox: on or off. Shows what hit you when you die.

Actions

These rows open something when you confirm them.

  • Watch cutscenes: the intro, which is always available, and the ending, once you beat the game.
  • Scoreboard: keeps the top 10 on this device.
  • Enter savecode: gives back your score and cleared stages from the code shown after a stage clear or game over. See Saving on the Player page.

Toxic Mode (12+)

Toxic Mode is off by default and meant for players aged 12 and up. When it's on, a voice makes fun of you every time you die, with rude words, and there is a different voice line for every way you can die. It is not for young players.

Elsewhere on the title screen

Some things the manual lists under Options now have their own rows on the title screen:

  • Costumes: new ships, one for each stage you clear. See the Player page for what each one does.
  • Alternative Game Modes: Speedrun Mode and Endless Mode, once you beat the game.
  • The title screen also has Stage Menu, Load Game (when you have a save), Achievements, Read the Manual and Quit Game. Enter the Outer Space appears once you have cleared all four stages.
