Crystals

Eight aspects, one White Universal Crystal. Every colour in the game is one of them, and every colour tells you the rules.

Warm (yours)Life, Spirit, Creativity, Speed
Cool (Delta's)Control, Resistance, Matter, Power
OriginThe White Universal Crystal

The story

At first, there was nothing. Then, from the smallest known particles, the White Universal Crystal formed, made of eight aspects: Speed, Creativity, Spirit, Life, Control, Resistance, Matter and Power.

A thousand years later, a character named Delta appeared and got bored of living in monochrome. He struck the crystal: the last four aspects broke away, while the others held fast. Delta seized the Crystal of Power. The detached crystals tried to reunite, but there was no power to do so, so Delta captured them too and forged them into the "Crystal of Cool Colours", spinning it up so it would finally create colourful things.

The original, the "Crystal of Warm Colours", eventually couldn't take it anymore and shattered. Delta locked the Crystal of Life away in a storage room, which became the first stage, where it still gives off healing orbs. The other three ended up in the stages of three bosses. The Crystal of Spirit spewed out half its energy, which drifted for a while, absorbed a few healing orbs, and became the player. As the ambassador of all the crystals, you need to return them.

Warm colours: yours

  • Speed — The melee weapon, Line Slasher.
  • Creativity — Bounces off things: Bouncy Ball.
  • Spirit — You, your ship and your shots.
  • Life — Heals you. Every healing orb.

Cool colours: Delta's

Delta forged these four into one crystal. In the Finale, each sits inside a force field shaped like a spinning 4D shape.

  • Control — Breaks to any shot.
  • Resistance — Only a charged shot breaks it.
  • Matter — Never breaks. Don't touch it.
  • Power — Hits you twice as hard.
