How to start it

Endless Mode and Speedrun Mode share the Alternative Game Modes row of the title screen, which appears once you beat the game. Pick Endless Mode with the up and down arrows and press Z or Space to play (on a phone, tap it). X goes back. The line under the list shows its rules and your best round: 3 HP, 1 life.

How it works

It goes round after round until you fall.

  • Each round has 4 phases of random attacks, then a boss.
  • It gets a little harder every round.
  • You have 3 HP and one life, whatever the difficulty.
  • You can use all the weapons. Switch with V.

Healing

A heal orb appears before every phase and boss, and you also get one for every 100 damage you deal.

Records

Your best round and score are kept on this device.
