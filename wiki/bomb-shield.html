Weapon

Bomb Shield

The Bomb Shield: Six hollow spheres orbit your ship; throw them at what is in your way.

OriginMade with the Cyber Shotgun
Made withCyber Shotgun, hold 1.5 s
Spheres6, each blocks 1 hit
Damage2 per sphere, up to 12

How it works

  • Defence: Six hollow spheres orbit your ship. Each one blocks one hit, whatever hit you.
  • Throwing: Move to throw it the way you face. Hold C to throw one way and move another.
  • Damage: Every sphere that touches cyan or dark blue deals 2 damage, up to 12 in all. Purple, pink and walls don't stop it.
  • Limit: One shield at a time.

Tip: Raise it, walk up to a tough enemy, then throw it point-blank.

Colour rules

Cyan is destroyed by any shot, dark blue only by a charged shot, purple can't be destroyed, and pink can't be destroyed and hits for 2.
