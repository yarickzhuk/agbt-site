Crystal of Life

The Crystal of Life is one of the eight aspects of the White Universal Crystal. It is one of the four warm crystals, which are yours.

GroupWarm colours (yours)
ColourGreen
ParticlesElectrons and gluons
WhereShape Limbo
In the gameHeals you. Every healing orb.

ExtractionFrom the top, nothing in the way

Story

Delta locked the Crystal of Life in a safe guarded by a Trap Circle. That safe became the first stage, Shape Limbo.

It keeps giving off electrons: the heal orbs.

In the game

The colour green means: Heals you. Every healing orb.

Extraction

Every stage ends with its crystal. Shoot it 9 times to extract it. Any shot works, and it never hurts you. Meanwhile the Mothership closes in, and its edge hits for 3. It leaves once you're done.

In Shape Limbo, it closes in from the top, nothing in the way. The stage boss is Trap Circle.
