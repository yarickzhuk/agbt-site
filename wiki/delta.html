Delta

Delta is the character who broke the White Universal Crystal. He is the final boss of the game, and once you beat the game you can play as him through his costume. He is one character in both roles.

Who he is

A thousand years after the White Universal Crystal formed, Delta appeared. He got bored of living in monochrome, so he struck the crystal. The last four aspects broke away, while the others held fast. Delta seized the Crystal of Power, captured the other detached crystals, and forged all four into the "Crystal of Cool Colours", spinning it up so it would finally create colourful things. (See Crystals.)

Appearance

Delta looks like the Greek letter delta: a plain white triangle. White is the colour of the monochrome he was tired of.

Boss fight

Delta is the final boss. HP: 100.

  • He fights with your own weapons.
  • He takes half damage from charged hits.
  • He answers every hit with a dash and a slash.

The manual explains the mirroring: the particles that form Delta's body are the dark opposites of yours. Photinos mirror your photons and positrons mirror your electrons, which is why he mirrors your every weapon. Behind him and every hazard in your way lies the negative void made of subons, particles that exist below zero.

Playing as Delta

Beat the game to wear his costume. It is a challenge, not a boost:

  • No colours.
  • Every sound is backwards.
  • The screen is mirrored.
  • No passive. He doesn't need one.

In the manual's Quantum Codex, mirror matter is the particle that flips your screen, sound and colours backwards. See the other ships on the Player page.

Tip: learn each hazard by its shape, since colours won't tell you the rules.
