Crystal of Spirit

The Crystal of Spirit is one of the eight aspects of the White Universal Crystal. It is one of the four warm crystals, which are yours.

Story

The Crystal of Spirit was flung the farthest when the White Universal Crystal broke. It spewed out about half its energy.

A drifting piece of that energy met some electrons and became you, the player. That is why you can heal.

In the game

The colour yellow means: You, your ship and your shots.

Extraction

Every stage ends with its crystal. Shoot it 9 times to extract it. Any shot works, and it never hurts you. Meanwhile the Mothership closes in, and its edge hits for 3. It leaves once you're done.

In Cyber Zoo, it closes in from the left, with 4 laser gates. The stage boss is Kangunroo.

Shoot a gate's cyan block to turn its beam off.
