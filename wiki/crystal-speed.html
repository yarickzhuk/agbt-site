Crystal of Speed

The Crystal of Speed is one of the eight aspects of the White Universal Crystal. It is one of the four warm crystals, which are yours.

Story

The Crystal of Speed ended up in Occültacresburg.

A piece cracked off as it landed: the Line Slasher.

In the game

The colour red means: The melee weapon, Line Slasher.

Extraction

Every stage ends with its crystal. Shoot it 9 times to extract it. Any shot works, and it never hurts you. Meanwhile the Mothership closes in, and its edge hits for 3. It leaves once you're done.

In Occültacresburg, it closes in from below, with 8 bombs. The stage boss is Plasmadrache.
