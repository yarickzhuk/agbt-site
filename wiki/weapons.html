Gameplay

Weapons

Each stage gives you its own weapon. Clear the stage to keep it. Press V to switch weapons once you have more than one, and hold C to keep facing one way while you move.

Colours decide what hurts

The colour of a thing tells you the rules: cyan is destroyed by any shot, dark blue is hurt only by a charged shot, purple can't be destroyed so you dodge it, and pink can't be destroyed and hits for 2. Green orbs heal you, and yellow is you.

Charge Cannon

The weapon from Shape Limbo.

  • Tap: a bullet, 1 damage.
  • Hold and let go: a torpedo, 3 damage. It pierces purple and kills dark blue.
  • Keep holding: the ship glows, then pulses. Let go then for a supercharge, 10 damage. It stops on the first boss it hits.

Tip: save supercharges for bosses, and use torpedoes on rows of enemies.

Cyber Shotgun

The weapon from Cyber Zoo.

  • Tap: 3 fast pellets, 2 damage each. Pellets kill dark blue without charging, but break on purple, so shoot around it.
  • It takes 0.6 seconds to reload after every volley, and you move at half speed with it.
  • Hold 1.5 seconds and let go: the Bomb Shield (below).

Tip: point-blank, all 3 pellets hit.

Bomb Shield

Made with the Cyber Shotgun. Six hollow spheres orbit your ship.

  • Each sphere blocks one hit, whatever hit you.
  • Move to throw it the way you face. Hold C to throw one way and move another.
  • Every sphere that touches cyan or dark blue deals 2 damage, up to 12 in all. Purple, pink and walls don't stop it.
  • You can have one shield at a time.

Tip: raise it, walk up to a tough enemy, then throw it point-blank.

Bouncy Ball

The weapon from Crazy Carnival.

  • Tap: a ball that bounces off what it hits, killing what it can. It ends at the screen edge.
  • Hold and let go: an orange ball that kills dark blue and bounces off the edges too. Against purple and walls, it just ricochets.
  • You can't have more balls on screen than your max HP. A ball hits the same target once per 0.6 seconds.

Tip: fire into a crowd or a corridor and let the bounces do the work.

Line Slasher

The weapon from Occültacresburg.

  • Tap: a slash. Its reach is 1.5, in a 120° arc in front of you. It does 1 damage but counts as charged, so it kills dark blue. It can't cut purple or reach through solid walls.
  • Hold and let go: a dash. Nothing can hurt you while dashing, and it hits what you pass through. You can slash in the middle of a dash.

Tip: dash through a hazard to dodge it and hit what's behind it at once.
