Crystal of Matter

The Crystal of Matter is one of the eight aspects of the White Universal Crystal. It is one of the four cool crystals, which Delta took.

GroupCool colours (Delta's)
ColourPurple

WhereFinale: the 24-cell

Story

Delta forged the four cool crystals into one. In the Finale, each of them hides in a 4D shape. Matter hides in the 24-cell.

In the game

The colour purple means: Never breaks. Don't touch it.
