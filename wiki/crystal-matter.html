Crystal of Matter

The Crystal of Matter is one of the eight aspects of the White Universal Crystal. It is one of the four cool crystals, which Delta took.

Story

Delta forged the four cool crystals into one. In the Finale, each of them sits inside a force field shaped like a spinning 4D shape. The force field of Matter is the 24-cell.

In the game

The colour purple means: Never breaks. Don't touch it.

Force field: the 24-cell

The Finale's phases 2 to 5 are the four force fields of the cool crystals. Each is a spinning 4D shape, and its lines hurt to touch. The gaps between the lines are safe.

  • 24-cell: There is no HP to beat: survive for a minute.
  • Behaviour: It drifts around.

Tip: a shot that slips through a gap and hits the crystal inside still counts.
