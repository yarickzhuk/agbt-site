Showman

Showman is the boss of Crazy Carnival, a top-hatted boss who is fought in the Cruel Clownhouse, a maze full of clown targets. He is the same character in both of his forms: the stage boss, and Showman Delta in the boss rush.

Appearance

Showman is built from simple shapes: a tall top hat with a wide flat brim, a big round body that is also his face, slanted angry brows, two four-pointed star eyes, a zigzag mouth, and a bow tie at the bottom.

Boss fight: normal form (Cruel Clownhouse)

The fight has two parts. HP: 15.

  1. The maze. You have 30 seconds to pop the 5 clown targets. Pop all 5 and Showman turns cyan. Miss even one and he is orange, and nothing can hurt him.
  2. The show. A force field shrinks in from the edges. Touch it and you lose 1 HP and go back to the maze. Meteors and a dark-blue clown join in. Touching Showman costs 1 HP.

Tip: Bouncy Balls race down the maze corridors. If he's orange, let the force field catch you and try again.

Boss fight: Delta form (Showman Delta)

Showman Delta is fight 2 of the boss rush, in Outer Space, stage 2. The arena is a big maze, and Showman waits at the end of its longest path. You have 20 seconds, shown at the top right. HP: 10.

  • Run out of time and you lose 1 HP, and the clock restarts.
  • Nothing gets through the walls: no shot, no dash.
  • He is small, never moves, and hurts to touch.
  • You don't have to reach him. Shoot once you have a clear line.

Tip: learn the route. A supercharge takes half his HP in one hit.
