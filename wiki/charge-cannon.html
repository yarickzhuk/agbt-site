Weapon

Charge Cannon

The Charge Cannon: Tap to shoot, hold for a torpedo, hold longer for a supercharge.

Given byShape Limbo
Tap1 damage
Torpedo3 damage
Supercharge10 damage

How it works

  • Tap: A bullet, 1 damage.
  • Hold and let go: A torpedo, 3 damage. It pierces purple and kills dark blue.
  • Keep holding: The ship glows, then pulses. Let go then for a supercharge, 10 damage. It stops on the first boss it hits.

Tip: Save supercharges for bosses, and use torpedoes on rows of enemies.

Colour rules

Cyan is destroyed by any shot, dark blue only by a charged shot, purple can't be destroyed, and pink can't be destroyed and hits for 2.
