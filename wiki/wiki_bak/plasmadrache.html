Plasmadrache

Plasmadrache is the boss of Occültacresburg, a winged dragon that spits flames and bites. Whatever form it takes, it is the same character.

Appearance

Plasmadrache is a large dragon drawn as blue and cyan outlines on black, with magenta eyes and a horned head. It has two big spiked wings, a cyan striped belly, and a long cyan tail ending in an arrow-shaped tip. Glowing diamond-shaped crystals sit around its wings, and rings of light mark its claws and feet.

Boss fight

Plasmadrache is fought in Occültacresburg, the stage that teaches the Line Slasher. It spits flaming fireballs at the player, and it also bites: never hit Plasmadrache while its mouth is open. Beating it gives you the Line Slasher for good.
