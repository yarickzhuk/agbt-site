The Player

You are a piece of the Crystal of Spirit. Its energy drifted for a while, absorbed a few healing orbs, and woke up as a little yellow ship. As the ambassador of all the crystals, it is your job to take them back.

OriginCrystal of Spirit
ColourYellow
Normal difficulty3 HP, 3 lives

Appearance

The player is a small yellow triangle with a smaller triangle outlined inside it, drawn in the same flat line-art style as the rest of the game. Yellow always means you. Your spare lives are shown as small yellow triangles next to your HP.

Health, lives and difficulty

Pick the difficulty on the title screen with the left and right arrows.

  • Easy: 4 HP, 3 lives. Lives refill on a clear, and a heal orb drops for every 100 damage you deal.
  • Normal: 3 HP, 3 lives.
  • Hard: 2 HP, 2 lives. Unlocked by beating the game.

When you run out of lives, Give Up starts a new run, and your cleared stages stay.

Health orbs and score

Green orbs heal 1 HP. Touch them, don't shoot them. The minus on an orb is an electron (see Crystals). Orbs appear between phases and fade after about 12 seconds, and each one is worth 1 point.

You score for what you destroy. Purple and pink things can't be destroyed, so they give no points. Your total carries from stage to stage, and at one million it reads "M".

Saving

  • Press Shift any time in a stage to save, and Enter to go back to your save. Load Game on the title screen does the same. The save stays after you close the game.
  • Without a save, a new tab starts from zero.
  • Every stage clear and game over shows a code. In Options, Enter Savecode gives your score and cleared stages back.
  • Beating the game saves by itself.
