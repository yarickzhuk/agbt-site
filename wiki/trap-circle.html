Trap Circle

Trap Circle is the boss of Shape Limbo, a spinning ring that closes in on you. It is the same character in both of its forms: the stage boss, and Trap Circle Delta in Outer Space.

Appearance

Trap Circle is a ring, not a creature: two thin concentric circles in pink, with a single cyan sector on it. It spins around the player, who sits in the middle, and it closes in.

Boss fight: normal form

A spinning ring closes in on you. Only its cyan sector takes damage. HP: 10.

  • Every hit springs the ring back open.
  • If it closes all the way, you lose 1 HP.
  • It closes faster as its HP drops.

Tip: follow the cyan sector around. A supercharge ends it in one hit.

Boss fight: Delta form (Trap Circle Delta)

Trap Circle Delta is the fight of Outer Space, stage 1 (the boss rush is stage 2). The ring has 9 segments and is slower. HP: 120.

  • Cyan ×3: 10 HP each, any shot hurts them.
  • Dark blue ×3: 30 HP each, charged hits only.
  • Pink ×3: can't be destroyed, and hits for 2.

A destroyed segment leaves a gap, and a hit springs the ring open. If the ring closes, you lose 1 HP. Destroy all the cyan and dark blue segments to win.

Tip: keep shooting so it never closes, and keep an eye on the pink.
