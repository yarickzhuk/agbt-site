Crystal of Creativity

The Crystal of Creativity is one of the eight aspects of the White Universal Crystal. It is one of the four warm crystals, which are yours.

Story

The Crystal of Creativity ended up in Crazy Carnival.

You absorb the quarks it gives off: they are the Bouncy Balls.

In the game

The colour orange means: Bounces off things: Bouncy Ball.

Extraction

Every stage ends with its crystal. Shoot it 9 times to extract it. Any shot works, and it never hurts you. Meanwhile the Mothership closes in, and its edge hits for 3. It leaves once you're done.

In Crazy Carnival, it closes in from the right, with 4 laser gates. The stage boss is Showman.

Shoot a gate's cyan block to turn its beam off.
